# Future national planning applications

17 November 2026. Owner requests this design; **deferred beyond initial simulation**. D70 proposes the lifecycle and limits. Read with [Government](GOVERNMENT.md), [Data/security](DATA-AND-SECURITY.md) and [Demand](CLIMATE-AND-DEMAND.md).

Applications may propose residential expansion, commercial districts, industrial developments, new connections or additional supply capacity. An application contains stable country/era/application ID, definition/version, proposing settlement/industry, location/footprint, created/expiry simulation steps, estimated population/jobs/development benefits, hourly/seasonal demand range in MW, expected annual MWh at a stated model version, connection node/voltage/route requirements, funding responsibility, prerequisites and possible approval/environmental consequences. Estimates have assumptions and confidence; they are not current demand or a guaranteed benefit.

Proposed lifecycle: Draft → Submitted → UnderReview → Approved or Conditional or Deferred or Rejected; Approved/Conditional may become Funded → Building → ReadyToCommission → Active. Expired/Withdrawn/Cancelled are explicit terminal alternatives before activation. Deferral stores a next-review simulation step, not a new proposal ID. Conditional approval stores measurable predicates (for example funded feeder and forecast firm capacity) and expiry; it is not permission to operate before those predicates pass.

Approval grants permission only. It does not create electricity, promise dispatch priority, debit an unspecified amount or immediately grow population. A separate authorized funding/construction operation reserves money and sites once. At commissioning recheck ownership, completed route, topology revision, legal capacity/service feasibility and all conditions against current state. Capacity reservations for competing proposals must be explicit, bounded and released on expiry/cancellation; a sum of nameplate capacities is not a feasibility check. Commit development and new demand together at a simulation boundary using the application ID as an idempotency key. Interrupted commissioning cannot duplicate residents, industry or connections.

Residential activation changes population/housing according to later growth rules; commerce/industry activation creates bounded schedules/components consumed by DemandModel. A capacity-only application has no new demand unless it includes a commissioned consumer. Political consequences are proposed transparent approval-pressure deltas with caps/cooldowns, not hidden punishment for deferral or an implemented election system. Benefits are not a second automatic income stream; any tax or subsidy design remains separately gated.

Staff may later rank applications, prepare forecasts and automatically approve within explicit player policy, budget and risk limits. Default automation is off. Never override reserved operating cash, ownership, environmental constraints or player rejection; show an audit reason and allow cancellation before irreversible commitments. No AI agent per household and no unbounded application spawning.

Proposed cap: 16 open applications per country; aggregate/reject new generation at cap with a visible explanation, preserve active commitments and compact completed records. Event/clock keys prevent reconnect rerolls. Tests: duplicate approval/commissioning, stale network quote, two proposals sharing a constrained feeder, conditional expiry, insufficient funds, staff limits, deletion of a required asset, cancellation/refunds and load/resume. Exact proposal cadence, growth/political values, funding sources and UI placement under Government's approved tabs remain unresolved. Implementation should be a separately authorized P7 extension after construction, forecasting and persistence exist.
