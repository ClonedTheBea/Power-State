## Scope and fleet progression

Recommend the first operations proof as a **separate fake training country** with a clear scenario identity, not the normal starter or a changed saved country. Its initial fleet is installed as scenario data, not an opening grant or a free migration award. Reuse Native icons, infrastructure markers and the existing server-owned graph/coordinate contracts. Three generators feed a common 10 MW substation with separate rated spurs; two demand buses provide a constrained branch. Connections have explicit endpoints and one abstract compatibility tier; visual line crossings never create junctions. Grid operation and dispatch are server-owned, independent of visible map cells.

| Unit | Capacity | Variable / MWh | Fixed / On hour | Controls and role |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Existing gas | 5 MW | 80 Credits | 10 Credits | Auto under cap0–5 MW; main flexible supply; all old values preserved |
| Wind farm | 2 MW | 0 Credits | 6 Credits | Enabled/curtailed, output cap; weather potential bounds actual output |
| Fast peaker | 2 MW | 110 Credits | 2 Credits | Off or On/ready under cap; emergency capacity with small standby cost |

Off pays zero in this first model, including wind. On fixed cost applies even at zero dispatch; no double fuel charge. An On dispatchable unit with cap0 is deliberately withheld, not counted as immediately usable reserve. No start charge, ramp delay, wear, storage, exports, taxes, grants, insurance, emergency borrowing or player pricing yet. These simplify operations and must be labelled, not marketed as physical plant simulation.

Possible later progression for existing countries: retain the district as the first purchase, then a surveyed wind connection, then peaker/network reinforcement as more demand is separately approved. **Candidate wind price8,000 Credits; peaker3,000 Credits**, each including its surveyed connection and preserving the250 buffer. These are balance hypotheses only; sites, safe routes and generation opportunities require another construction approval. At 30% capacity factor, fully useful wind replaces0.6 MW gas:48 Credits/hour fuel saving minus6 fixed =42/hour, or1,008/day;8,000 pays back in190.48 simulated hours (190.48 active minutes at the existing clock). Curtailment or congestion lengthens payback. At10% useful factor the saving is10/hour; at zero use wind loses6/hour. No guaranteed yield or income promise.

The peaker is not a money printer: when serving2 MW otherwise unmet, incremental sales240 minus220 fuel minus2 fixed =18/hour. A3,000 cost takes166.67 hours of continuous full useful operation, much longer at occasional use. Its purpose is reliability and future capacity, not immediate payback. Existing5 MW already covers the approved4.764 MW peak: additional firm capacity should not be sold as necessary for today's starter. Demand expansion needs its own content approval, not an invisible multiplier or automatic growth.

## Explicit electricity abstraction and solver boundary

This is an **energy-routing strategy model**, not AC power flow. Integer1 kW flows, zero losses, hard equipment ratings, independent connected islands, no voltage, reactive power, impedance, inertia, frequency, transient cascades or grid-forming claims. Wind-only islands can be served in this abstraction. Do not display fabricated Hz or a physical stability score.

Keep GridSolver and DistrictSolver/composition2 unchanged for historical fixtures and saves. New `FleetNetworkModel` and `FleetSolver` would accept immutable validated graph, generator availability/caps/costs and sink requests. `OperationsStep` would orchestrate commands → availability/demand → funded bounds → solve → invariant checks → exact accounting → one atomic candidate. No service calls or financial mutation inside the solver. Topology compiled/cache keyed by topology and solver version, never by GUI state.

Proposed bounds:4 sources,8 sinks,32 physical nodes,48 physical links, total100,000 kW,8 countries/server. No unlimited procedural graph. Share a physical edge's one capacity in both directions, retain substation throughput gates, and derive actual source output from routed flow. Parallel links are separate physical assets; reverse residual edges must undo allocations. Reject unknown versions, duplicate IDs, malformed references, unsupported node roles, negative/NaN/fractional-kW values and cap overflow before solving.

Allocation policy needs explicit approval: within each connected island, maximize the minimum delivered/requested fraction among positive-demand buses, then progressively fill the remaining feasible buses after bottlenecked buses freeze. Zero-demand buses do not participate. A disconnected island with no source gets zero without dragging other islands to zero. A branch-limited customer freezes at its reachable fraction; it must not hold all remaining customers at that fraction. Once this fair delivery vector is established, minimize total variable generation cost while preserving it. Fixed On costs are already determined by player state and are not optimization coefficients. Equal-cost source ties use stable IDs; any final sub-kW rounding tie rotates by persisted simulation step, not join order. No essential-customer priority in v1.

Prototype the progressive-filling feasibility procedure using integer target vectors, bounded binary searches and residual max flow, then min-cost flow for the chosen demands. Proposed fraction resolution1/1000, with exact1kW remainder allocation and a documented <=one fraction quantum plus1kW fairness error. At most8 freeze rounds and10 fraction searches/round. Keep explicit work accounting across **all** feasibility and cost passes: proposal2,000,000 edge relaxations/step, not a fresh allowance for each pass. Stop the entire candidate without billing on exhaustion. This is a proposed algorithm and budget, not a proven complexity/performance result. If frozen-set identification, fair rounding or residual cost correctness cannot meet these bounds, return to this approval gate; do not quietly revert to greedy sink priority.

Prototype acceptance target: p95<=2ms per small-fleet solve, eight-country simulation CPU<=16ms per scheduled batch on the recorded Studio machine, no step>10ms without diagnostics. Existing legacy200,000-operation bound remains unchanged. Hard new operation exhaustion suspends only the affected country, emits a recoverable diagnostic and settles no partial step. Hardware measurements and pathological fixtures must justify final limits before rollout.

## Dispatch, demand, wind and reserve

Commands set owned-generator On/Off and a cap in integer100kW increments (server range checks); the solver may choose any integer-kW output below that cap. Auto economic dispatch uses cheap available sources first **only where routing allows**, with maximum/fair delivery taking precedence over cost. Capping a unit is not a request to generate unwanted power. Curtailment = potential minus routed output; never count it as production, sales or reserve guaranteed by fuel.

Existing countries retain the exact P2b demand function and district formula. For the isolated operator scenario only, propose a versioned scenario demand envelope3–6 MW across two buses, with an evening increase forecast ahead of time. This is training content, not a balance amendment to the1,000-resident country. Do not save it as normal generated-country demand.

Proposed wind v1: hourly knot values in integer permille0–800 from a new namespaced versioned hash of existing country seed, region ID and simulated hour; piecewise integer-linear interpolation using completed simulation step. Nameplate times factor floors to integer kW. Region correlation is one shared regional series in this small proof; no private random draw per reconnect or frame. Hourly knots mean a60-step transition, not unbounded flickering. The0–800 envelope allows calm and headroom; physical wind speed/cut-out equations are deliberately deferred. Pin/hash golden vectors before accepting this new cosmetic-independent generator model. It must not modify the approved terrain/climate hashes or versions.

Forecast five sample points at now/+15/+30/+45/+60 simulated minutes, recalculated once per15 completed steps or accepted control/topology change. Server uses the same deterministic demand/wind versions. Show “forecast under current controls”; future faults/control changes can invalidate it. No income or reserved funds from forecasts. Forecast preview must not advance actual RNG, clock, energy, finance or lease. Do not run a full solver on every frame; bounded approximate capacity warnings are explicitly labelled, with at most one optional diagnostic solve per sample within a separate budget.

Display separately: installed MW, presently available MW, actual generation, customer demand/delivery, and **ready dispatchable headroom** (sum of On, available, cap-constrained dispatchable capacity minus its actual dispatch) by island. Wind potential is not firm reserve. National sums can hide stranded spare capacity: show island shortage/congestion alongside totals. Proposed advisory target =max(0.5MW,10% current island demand); it is a gameplay warning, not an N-1 security guarantee. “Available headroom” is not proven deliverable reserve through bottlenecks; label route-limited situations and defer a formal contingency reserve calculation.

## Example operating scenario and arithmetic

Values below are proposed scenario points for one simulated hour each; they are not changed P2b output or new test results. All plants On unless indicated. Sale price120; revenue is delivered electricity only.

| Situation | Dispatch (wind/gas/peaker MW) | Delivered / unmet MW | Sales | Variable cost | Fixed cost | Net Credits/hour |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Moderate demand3, wind1.2; peaker Off | 1.2 / 1.8 / 0 | 3 / 0 | 360 | 144 | 16 | 200 |
| Evening demand6, wind0.2; peaker Off | .2 / 5 / 0 | 5.2 / .8 | 624 | 400 | 16 | 208 |
| Operator enables peaker before evening peak | .2 / 5 / .8 | 6 / 0 | 720 | 488 | 18 | 214 |
| Same demand6 but total route capacity4 | .2 / 3.8 / 0 | 4 / 2 | 480 | 304 | 18 | 158 |

The forecast warns wind is falling while demand rises. The player enables the peaker, observes the shortage disappear and sees its small fixed/variable expense. If the route is constrained instead, enabling more generation cannot solve it: show the bottleneck and explain that a later reinforcement project is needed. Do not offer an unimplemented purchase as a working repair. With gas unexpectedly unavailable in a **test fixture**, wind.2+peaker2 supply2.2 of6; recovery restores routing at the next step. Random outage scheduling/repair spending remain deferred, while disabled/isolated/unfunded status is immediately understandable.

Switching On/Off every few ticks cannot avoid fixed cost for intervals served. Online state and costs are sampled together at the step boundary. Reserve is informational and gives no bonus money. More reliability can increase delivery revenue, but reserve alone earns nothing. This avoids creating busywork for rewards.

## Funding, money and per-generator attribution

Retain one Bank, integer hundredths, 1/60-hour steps, denominator60,000, bounded carried remainders, unchanged120 sales, gas80+10, existing grant identity and250 optional-spend buffer. Bill each source's **actual** generated kW-minutes at its pinned variable price; fixed On step numerator = fixedCreditsPerHour×100000. Sales numerator = totalDeliveredKW×12000. No forced minimum production, exports, losses or storage means summed source output equals total delivery.

Per-unit meters: generatedKwMinutes, onlineSteps, variablePostedMinor/remainder, fixedPostedMinor/remainder. Post-cutover national expenses are sums of posted per-unit expenses; do not also round/bill a second aggregate expense carry. Existing gas carries transfer exactly; new units start at zero when durably commissioned. Rounding may differ from rounding one pooled fleet category, which is why ledger4 is explicit. National eligible sales retain one carry to avoid source attribution paying duplicate revenue. Unit UI shows output and expense; optional “sales contribution” is a non-ledger analytic proportional allocation whose residual reconciles to national sales. It must not be described as traced electrons through meshed lines.

Proposed funding policy for Operations mode: reserve worst-case one-step gross costs for every requested On unit using current available/capped kW and carried liabilities, before solving; no expected-sales credit. If the combined reservation is unaffordable, **suspend the economic step with Funds unavailable**, allow server-validated Off/cap commands, and retry after the player's cheaper configuration. No arbitrary ID-based unit shedding, negative Bank or partial financial step. This differs from legacy single-generator auto-Off and requires N3/N5 approval. Keep legacy behavior unchanged for legacy mode. A public-release, non-Robux insolvency recovery design remains a prerequisite; this proposal does not introduce a bailout. Conservative overreservation may suspend a feasible cheaper dispatch and must be playtested, not hidden.

## Control-room view

Reuse AppController, theme, responsive navigation, focus/actions and Native map. A new Operations route would summarize rather than replace the map: on desktop a compact national KPI row, fleet table and forecast strip beside the map; on tablet a fleet panel above/beside a collapsible inspector; mobile top cards for delivered/demand and ready reserve, a virtualized fleet list with44px controls and separate Forecast/Alerts panels. Preserve map camera, selected ID and focus when changing panels. Avoid more decorative map instances: existing map budget600 remains a gate.

Fleet row: name/technology, On/Off/unavailable reason, available/actual MW, cap, current cost/hour and Locate. Selecting opens an inspector with step-boundary confirmation/pending/error state and accessible cap stepper; never optimistic authoritative output. National cards distinguish generation from delivery, installed from available, and readiness from a route guarantee. Forecast uses text plus small sparklines, not color alone. Important alerts: unserved customers, congestion, low headroom, unfunded operation, unavailable generator, save uncertainty. Bound/coalesce by reason+asset, maximum20 active entries; acknowledge only hides emphasis, never clears the physical condition. This is operational feedback, not R4 news/events.

Government compatibility is an interface boundary: a future server-approved policy can supply a versioned cost/availability/demand modifier at the next step. No current policy tabs, elections, staff, laws, emissions taxes or dispatch-priority privileges are implemented. Storage needs its own source/sink exclusivity, efficiency and conservation accounting; imports need a real connector. Do not squeeze those into this lossless domestic-only contract.
